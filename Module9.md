# Module 9 Knowledge Check


### **Q1.**
Which of the following is _not_ one of the four areas of the **Performance Efficiency** pillar of the AWS Well-Architected Framework?

**Answer:**  
**Traceability**

---

### **Q2.**
Which of the following is a principle when designing cloud-based systems?

**Answer:**  
**Assume everything will fail**

---

### **Q3.**
Which of the following are pillars of the AWS Well-Architected Framework? _(Choose three)_

**Answer:**  
- **Security**
- **Operational Excellence**
- **Cost Optimization**

---

### **Q4.**
Which design principles are recommended when considering **Performance Efficiency**? _(Choose two)_

**Answer:**  
- **Use serverless architecture**
- **Democratize advanced technologies**

---

### **Q5.**
AWS Trusted Advisor provides insight regarding which five categories of an AWS account?

**Answer:**  
**Performance, cost optimization, security, fault tolerance, service limits**

---

### **Q6.**
What is the focus of the **Sustainability** pillar of the AWS Well-Architected Framework?

**Answer:**  
**Minimize the environmental impacts of running cloud workloads**

---

### **Q7.**
After you move to the AWS Cloud, you want to ensure that the right security settings are in place. Which online tool can assist in security compliance?

**Answer:**  
**AWS Trusted Advisor**

---

### **Q8.**
Which of the following is a measure of your system’s ability to provide functionality when desired by the user?

**Answer:**  
**Reliability**

---

### **Q9.**
What is defined as the ability for a system to remain operational even if some of the components of that system fail?

**Answer:**  
**Fault tolerance**

---

### **Q10.**
Which of the following best describes a system that can withstand some measure of degradation, experiences minimal downtime, and requires minimal human intervention?

**Answer:**  
**Highly available**
